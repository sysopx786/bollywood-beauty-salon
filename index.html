Signature services

  • Eyebrow Threading

    Threading shapes the brow with a cotton thread. It is the service guests return for, from a quick eyebrow visit to a full face.

    Brows from $10

  • Bridal Makeup

    Bridal makeup and engagement makeup are printed prices on the current menu. The menu does not list a separate bridal-hair price or a packaged trial. Ask the salon what the $400 visit includes before the wedding week.

    Bridal makeup $400

  • Facials

    Facials here are cosmetic salon services. They are not medical treatment, and they are not a promise to change your skin color or cure acne.

    Facials from $35

  • Waxing

    Waxing is offered for the face and body. Full legs and Brazilian waxing are priced starting at the amount on the menu, because the visit can vary.

    Waxing from $5

  • Hair Color & Highlights

    Color is priced from the menu and finished to the hair in front of the stylist. Full and partial highlights start at the listed price. Extra foils are $5 each.

    Color from $25

  • Henna / Mehndi

    Henna is priced from the design. A small motif starts at $10. Bridal henna starts at $150. Henna used as a hair color starts at $35.

    Henna from $10
